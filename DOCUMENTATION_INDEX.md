## 🎨 CUSTOMIZATION TIPS

### Personalizing the Content

**Do:**
- ✅ Add your specific contributions to team work
- ✅ Include lessons you personally learned
- ✅ Mention challenges you specifically solved
- ✅ Add metrics from your development phase
- ✅ Customize the tone to match your voice

**Don't:**
- ❌ Claim solo work if it was team effort
- ❌ Exaggerate technical details
- ❌ Copy-paste without understanding
- ❌ Ignore team member contributions
- ❌ Use jargon without being able to explain it

### Adapting for Different Audiences

**For LinkedIn (Recruiters & Professionals):**
- Focus on impact and results
- Balance technical details with business value
- Include metrics and quantifiable achievements
- Emphasize teamwork and collaboration

**For GitHub (Developers & Engineers):**
- Deep technical implementation details
- Code architecture and design decisions
- Challenges and how you solved them
- Technologies and why you chose them

**For Portfolio (General Audience):**
- Visual demonstrations (screenshots, videos)
- Clear problem → solution narrative
- User benefits and impact
- Professional presentation

**For Resume (Brief & Scannable):**
- Bullet points with action verbs
- Quantifiable achievements
- Key technologies used
- Results and impact (3-4 bullets max)
